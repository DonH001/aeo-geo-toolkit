# AI Citation Check

A repeatable way to test whether a site is cited by AI search tools.

## Method
1. Use a fresh chat or a private window each time. Turn web search on.
2. Paste the query exactly. Do not mention your site.
3. Open the sources panel and note whether your domain appears.
4. For Google AI Mode, hover the inline links to see which passages are attributed to which site.
5. Repeat across tools: Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity.

## Query mix
Test three kinds of queries and keep results separate:
- **Tool or comparison queries** (your calculator, "A vs B"): usually your best chance.
- **How-to or explainer queries**: competitive, authority matters.
- **Commercial queries** ("best X near me"): dominated by established brands.

## Reading the results
- Cited in Google AI Mode but not in ChatGPT is common. They use different indexes and source preferences.
- Government and established publishers often win legal, medical and financial topics. New sites need authority signals over time.
- One session is a snapshot. Re-test weekly and compare.

## Limits
No tool can guarantee AI citations. This checklist measures; it does not promise.
